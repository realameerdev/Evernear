# Evernear

> **Some connections deserve another conversation.**

Evernear is a private AI experience built around memory, connection, and meaningful conversations.

It allows people to create a private AI recreation of someone meaningful to them — a parent, grandparent, childhood friend, partner, mentor, or someone they have lost contact with — using the memories, personality, voice description, and photos they choose to provide.

Evernear is not about replacing people.

It is about creating a quiet digital space where memories can become another conversation.

---

## What Evernear Does

Evernear lets users:

- Create private AI recreations of meaningful people
- Upload a photo as an appearance reference
- Describe the person's personality and voice
- Add personal memories, stories, phrases, and habits
- Have private text conversations
- Build separate experiences for multiple people
- Return to previous conversations
- Add and manage memories over time
- Keep everything inside a private personal workspace

The experience is designed to feel personal, calm, emotional, and human rather than like a traditional AI chatbot.

---

## Core Features

### Talk to Someone

Instead of treating the experience as “creating an AI,” Evernear lets users choose who they want to talk to.

The setup process collects only the information necessary to create a meaningful recreation:

- Photo
- Name
- Relationship
- Personality
- Voice description
- Memories

The process is intentionally short and simple.

---

### My People

A private space containing the people the user has chosen to keep close.

Each person has their own:

- Profile
- Memories
- Conversations
- Personality context
- Voice description
- Appearance reference

Users can search only within their own private people.

---

### Personal Conversations

Users can have conversations with each recreation through a dedicated conversation experience.

Each conversation maintains its own context and remains separate from other people.

---

### Memory System

Users can add meaningful information over time.

Examples include:

- Childhood memories
- Favourite sayings
- Stories
- Habits
- Important moments
- Personality traits
- Family memories

Evernear uses the information provided by the user as the foundation for the recreation.

It should never invent important memories or personal history.

---

### Private by Design

Evernear is designed around personal and sensitive memories.

There are no:

- Public profiles
- Global people directories
- Followers
- Social feeds
- Likes
- Public AI characters
- Marketplaces
- Leaderboards

Each user's people and conversations remain inside their private workspace.

---

## Honest AI

Evernear is an AI recreation.

It is not:

- Resurrection
- Actual communication with someone who has died
- A supernatural experience
- Proof that a person is still communicating

The AI should never falsely claim to actually be the person.

It can communicate naturally and warmly while remaining honest about what it is.

For example, it should never claim:

> “I am really your father.”

or:

> “I came back.”

The goal is emotional connection without deception.

---

## Living People

When creating a recreation of a living person, appropriate authorization should be required for realistic likeness or voice recreation.

Evernear should not facilitate unauthorized impersonation.

---

## Design Philosophy

Evernear follows a premium editorial visual direction.

The interface is designed to feel:

- Human
- Calm
- Emotional
- Cinematic
- Nostalgic
- Private
- Minimal
- Premium

The design uses generous whitespace, soft surfaces, realistic photography, subtle teal/cyan accents, restrained motion, and strong typography.

### Typography

**Manrope**

Manrope is used throughout the product for:

- Headings
- Body text
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Cards
- Forms
- Conversation interfaces

---

## Visual Language

Evernear intentionally avoids common “AI product” aesthetics.

No:

- ⭐ Star icons
- ✦ Sparkles
- ✧ AI sparkle symbols
- ✨ Magic effects
- Magic wand icons
- Robot illustrations
- Generic AI assistant graphics

Instead, the interface uses understated elements such as:

- Circles
- Dots
- Lines
- Arrows
- Message icons
- Microphone icons
- Image icons
- Simple audio waveforms
- Minimal geometric details
